Plumbing services in Roy, UT

Every service below is available across Roy and the nearby towns we cover. Pages marked by an emergency headline are the ones where problems tend to be urgent, so our 24/7 line applies. Planned work such as installations, repiping and water treatment is usually scheduled for a day that suits you.

If you are not sure which page matches your problem, start with the symptom. Water where it should not be belongs with leak detection or burst pipe repair, slow or backing-up drains belong with drain cleaning, and no hot water belongs with water heater repair.

Drain Cleaning

Clogged sinks, tubs, toilets and main lines cleared with cables and cameras.

Water Heater Repair

No hot water, leaks at the tank and pilot or element failures diagnosed first.

Water Heater Replacement

Failed or leaking tanks swapped for a correctly sized unit.

Leak Detection

Meter-running and wet-spot leaks traced with listening and imaging gear.

Sewer Line Repair

Cracked, rooted or belly sewer lines repaired or relined.

Burst Pipe Repair

Winter freeze breaks and failed fittings shut off and repaired.

Toilet Repair and Installation

Leaking bases, running tanks and new toilet installs.

Faucet Repair

Dripping faucets, failed valves and stuck shutoffs.

Garbage Disposal Repair and Installation

Jammed, leaking or dead disposals repaired or replaced.

Hydro Jetting

High-pressure water scouring for grease, scale and roots.

Sewer Line Cleaning

Main line blockages cleared and the line left flowing.

Sewer Inspection

Camera inspection that shows what is inside the line.

Water Heater Installation

Planned installs with the right venting, size and fittings.

Tankless Water Heater

Tankless installs, repairs and descaling flushes.

Repiping

Whole-house or partial repipes for failing supply lines.

Plumbing Installation

New fixtures, lines and remodel plumbing installed to plan.

Commercial Plumbing

Shops, offices and multi-unit buildings, including after-hours calls.

Sump Pump Installation and Repair

Failed pumps replaced and basement water managed.

Basement Waterproofing

Interior drainage and sealing for damp or seeping basements.

Water Softener and Filtration

Softeners and filters sized to real water test results.
