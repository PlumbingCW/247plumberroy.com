Emergency plumber for Riverdale, UT

Riverdale sits just east of Roy, so a plumbing emergency there is close to our base. Our 24/7 emergency line takes Riverdale calls at any hour. If water is running, close the main valve first. If a drain is backing up, stop using water. Then call and give your cross street so dispatch can start.

Riverdale is a small, mostly built-out city with a mix of mid century homes, newer subdivisions, apartments and commercial strips along the main roads. That mix means emergency calls vary, from a burst line in an older home to a water heater failure in an apartment.

Before we arrive

What to do while an emergency plumber heads to Riverdale

Water moves fast in a small city lot, so the first minutes count.

1

Find the shutoff.

The main valve is often near the water heater or where the line enters the house. Turn it clockwise until it stops.

2

Pause the drains.

If a tub or floor drain is gurgling, stop sinks, showers and the washer so nothing else pushes into the line.

3

Stay clear of wet power.

Keep out of rooms where water reaches outlets, and leave the panel alone unless the floor is dry.

4

Call with your address.

Ring the line and give the street, the cross street and whether the building is a house or an apartment.

Photos of the wet area help with an insurance claim later. Take them before cleanup if it is safe.

Local water

Riverdale's water comes from its own wells and from Weber Basin

Riverdale operates its own wells and also buys treated water from the Weber Basin Water Conservancy District. Public reporting about the city's system has noted that one of its wells has been idle because of sand, iron and manganese in the water. Those minerals are the kind that stain fixtures and can build up in water heaters.

Roughly half of the city's supply has been reported to come from Weber Basin, which lists hardness of 10 to 18 grains per gallon for its water. That is hard water. If you see white scale on fixtures or a rumbling water heater, see water heater repair and softener sizing.

Basements

Why Riverdale basements get extra attention

A local news report in 2011 described groundwater flooding in basements in a Riverdale neighborhood. That does not mean every Riverdale basement is at risk, but it is a reminder that the area sits near the Weber River and has a higher water table in places, especially in spring runoff.

If you have a sump pit, test the pump now and keep a backup plan. If you see seepage but no pit, basement waterproofing explains what is involved. A pump failure during a storm is an emergency, and the 24/7 line takes it.
