Before you call

What helps us help you faster

You do not need all of this, but any of it saves time.

  • Your street address or nearest cross street and your ZIP code.
  • What you see, hear or smell, and where in the house it is.
  • Whether water is still running and whether you could close the main shutoff.
  • The age of the water heater or fixture if the problem involves one.

If you smell natural gas, leave the building first and call the gas utility or 911, then call us.
