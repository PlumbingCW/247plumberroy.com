Sand Ridge

Plumbing service for Sand Ridge homeowners in Roy

Sand Ridge is the original settlement area of Roy. According to the Roy City 2023 General Plan, four families first settled what was then called Sand Ridge in 1873, long before the city took its present name.

Today the plan describes Sand Ridge as a northeast Roy neighborhood bounded by 4800 South and the Union Pacific tracks. It says the housing is primarily single-family homes from the 1950s and 1960s.

That makes this page about one thing: what a homeowner with a house of that age might reasonably want to know before calling a plumber.

The area

How to place Sand Ridge on a map

The plan lists Sand Ridge Park and the newer George E. Wahlen Park as the neighborhood parks. It also places Roy High School, Sand Ridge Junior High and the new Weber County Library in this part of the city.

Those anchors are useful when you call. A caller who says "near Roy High School" or "by Sand Ridge Park" gives dispatch a quicker picture than a street number alone. Exact boundaries are not drawn in the text we reviewed, so give your cross street.

Older homes

What a house from the 1950s or 1960s may need checked

A house built that long ago may be on its original layout but not on its original plumbing. Owners, previous owners and contractors may have replaced sections at different times, so two neighboring houses on one street can differ.

The practical step is to find out what you actually have. A plumber can look at the visible supply pipe near the water heater and at the main shutoff. A sewer camera inspection shows the condition of the main drain without digging.

If a drain backs up more than once, the drain cleaning page explains the usual first steps. If a camera finds a damaged section, the sewer line repair page covers the options.

Before you call

Notes to gather before phoning from Sand Ridge

Two minutes of preparation makes the first call more useful.

  • The street and the nearest cross street, or a landmark such as Roy High School or Sand Ridge Park.
  • Whether the problem is one fixture, several fixtures or the whole house.
  • Roughly how old the water heater is, which is printed on its label.
  • Whether you know of any past plumbing work, such as replaced pipe or a new water heater.
  • Photos of any stains, drips or standing water.

If you do not know an answer, say so. We will walk you through finding it.
