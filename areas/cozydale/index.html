Cozydale

Plumbing service for homes and rentals in Cozydale

The Roy City 2023 General Plan places Cozydale in mid-east Roy, bounded by 4800 South, 5600 South and the Union Pacific tracks. It says the neighborhood may be considered the heart of Roy.

The plan describes mid-century single-family areas next to the commercial core, along with two schools, city offices and a cemetery. A national neighborhood data site also notes some small apartment buildings among the houses.

That mix is the angle of this page. Cozydale includes owners and renters, so part of getting plumbing help is knowing who is responsible.

Who to call

Owner, landlord or tenant: who arranges the plumber

If you own your house, you arrange and pay for plumbing work. If you rent, your lease usually says whether the landlord or property manager handles repairs. Read it first, and report the problem in writing when you can.

Tenants should not pay a contractor on their own unless the landlord has agreed. If water is actively damaging the unit, shut off the supply and tell the landlord right away. A plumber can still explain what is wrong when you call.

Close to Downtown

Why location matters when you call from central Roy

Cozydale sits near Downtown Roy, where the city has been planning mixed-use change. Businesses, apartments and houses can share a block, so say what kind of building you are in.

A single house with a leaking fixture is a faucet repair or toilet repair question. A drain that backs up in several units is closer to a main line problem, and the drain cleaning page explains how that is approached.

Before you call

Details that help when you call from central Roy

  • Whether you own the property or rent it.
  • The cross street and the nearest major road, such as 5600 South or 1900 West.
  • Whether one fixture, one unit or the whole building is affected.
  • The age of the water heater if hot water is the problem.
  • The landlord or manager contact if you rent and the work needs approval.
Safety

First steps if water is running

If water is running and you cannot stop it, close the main shutoff, then tell whoever manages the building. In a multi-unit building the main may be shared, so ask the manager where it is before an emergency happens.

Keep electricity away from standing water, and if you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first.
