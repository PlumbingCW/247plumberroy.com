Arsenal Villa

Plumbing service for Arsenal Villa homeowners

Arsenal Villa is named in the Roy City 2023 General Plan as one of the first subdivisions built in Roy, together with Maplewood Estates. The plan describes it as a mid-century neighborhood in southeast Roy, bounded by 5600 South and the Union Pacific tracks.

It also says Arsenal Villa contains the southern end of Downtown and has generally good access and walkability. For plumbing that matters because older streets are straightforward to reach.

Anchors

Parks and schools that help you describe where you are

The plan lists Municipal Park and Municipal School in Arsenal Villa, and notes that the park's play equipment is slated for updates. These are useful landmarks when you give directions.

The plan gives rough bounds rather than a surveyed boundary, so we describe the area by its anchor streets. If your house is near 5600 South or the tracks, tell us the cross street and we will confirm.

Sewer and drains

Using a camera before deciding on a sewer repair

When a drain backs up repeatedly in an older neighborhood, guessing is expensive. A camera inspection shows whether the cause is a clog, a shifted joint, roots or a collapsed section. The sewer inspection page explains what you get.

If the camera finds damage, the sewer line repair page describes digging and trenchless options. If it only finds buildup, hydro jetting may clear the line.

Ask to see the recording and to have the depth of any problem marked on the surface. Keep the video, since it helps if you later compare quotes or sell the house.

Before you call

Preparing your call from southeast Roy

  • The cross street, or a landmark such as Municipal Park or Municipal School.
  • How many drains are slow and which ones, such as one sink or the lowest drain in the house.
  • Whether the problem is new or repeated, and any past plumber visits.
  • Whether you can see the sewer cleanout, usually a capped pipe near the foundation.
  • The age of the water heater, printed on its label.
Safety

First steps if a drain backs up

If a drain backs up, stop running water in the house. Every sink, shower and washer feeds the same line, so more water only pushes the problem further.

Do not pour chemical cleaners into a line that is already full, since the next person to open it may be exposed. Photograph the backup and call.
