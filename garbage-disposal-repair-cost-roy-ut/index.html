Short answer

What a garbage disposal repair really costs depends on four things

There is no honest single number for a disposal repair, because the job could be a thirty minute unjam or a full replacement. What you pay is set by what failed, whether the unit is worth saving, how hard it is to reach and what time of day you call. Anyone quoting a price without seeing the problem is guessing.

This guide explains each factor so you can judge a quote when you get one. If your disposal is leaking from the bottom right now, turn off the power and the supply under the sink and call. For the full service, see garbage disposal repair.

Quote check

How to judge a disposal quote

Four questions make most quotes easy to compare.

  1. 1

    Ask what failed.

    A jammed unit is a different job from a leaking housing. The answer should be specific.

  2. 2

    Ask about repair against replacement.

    If the unit is old and leaking, replacement is usually the sound choice, and the quote should say so.

  3. 3

    Ask what is included.

    Mounting hardware, a new drain connection, the dishwasher knockout and disposal of the old unit are all worth confirming.

  4. 4

    Ask about electrical needs.

    We connect a disposal to an existing outlet or switch. If a new circuit is needed, that is an electrician's job.

Decision

Repair or replace: a practical rule

Age and symptom decide most cases.

Worth repairing

  • A jam on a healthy motor.
  • A leak at a hose or gasket.
  • A unit that is only a few years old.
  • No cracks or corrosion on the housing.

Worth replacing

  • Water coming from the housing body.
  • A motor that hums but will not turn after unjamming.
  • An old unit that is noisy and slow.
  • Repeated repairs in a short time.

Many disposals last about a decade, though use and water quality change that.

Avoid repeat calls

What shortens a disposal's life

Most early failures come from what goes down the drain. Grease coats the grinding chamber and the pipe beyond it. Fibrous scraps like celery wrap around the shredder. Bones and fruit pits can jam a unit that is not built for them. Starchy food swells and packs into the trap arm.

Run a strong stream of cold water before, during and after use. Grind small amounts at a time. If the sink backs up even with the disposal working, the problem is beyond the unit. See drain cleaning for what comes next.

Planning

When to call and what to have ready

A short description of the symptom helps the plumber bring the right parts. Note whether the disposal hums, whether anything leaks and from where, and the brand name on the housing. A photo of the label helps. If you have a dishwasher connected to the disposal, mention it, since that adds a connection to check.

If you are in Riverdale or Clearfield, our dispatch covers you as well. We stay clear of electrical rewiring and will say so if the circuit is the problem.

Extra

Why a cheap disposal can cost more over time

A budget unit with a small motor is fine for a single person who cooks lightly. In a busy family kitchen it labors, jams and wears out sooner. Larger motors grind more finely, run quieter and tolerate tougher scraps, and they tend to come with longer warranties. The price difference is often smaller than the cost of a second installation visit.

Features also matter. Continuous feed units are common, while batch feed units only run with the stopper in place and add a layer of safety for homes with young children. Stainless grinding parts resist corrosion better than plated ones. Ask the plumber which fits your use and what each option adds to the clear price you are given.

Finally, think about the sink itself. A disposal puts weight on the flange and the sink bottom, and a thin stainless sink or an older composite one may need support. A good installer checks this before mounting rather than after the first leak.

Roy, UT local note: water and disposals

Roy City draws from four city wells and buys additional treated water from the Weber Basin Water Conservancy District, according to the city's 2024 water conservation plan. Weber Basin's published water quality report lists hardness of 10 to 18 grains per gallon for its water, and Roy blends sources, so ask Roy City for its current report or test your tap.

Why that matters for a disposal: scale from hard water builds inside the unit and in the trap arm beneath it, which makes the motor work harder and can shorten its life. Rinsing with plenty of cold water helps move waste through, and a softener reduces scale throughout the house. See water softener and filtration.
