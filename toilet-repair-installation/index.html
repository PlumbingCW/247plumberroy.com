Urgent first

A toilet that overflows or leaks needs quick action

If a toilet is overflowing, lift the tank lid and push the flapper closed, or turn the supply valve behind the toilet clockwise to stop the water. If it is leaking at the base, shut the same valve and put a towel around the base. Then call.

Our 24/7 line takes toilet emergencies. A toilet that will not stop running can waste a lot of water and raise your bill, so it is worth a call even if it is not flooding.

Process

What happens on a toilet repair or install visit

The goal is a toilet that flushes well and stays dry at the base.

  1. 1

    Stop the water and diagnose.

    The plumber shuts off the supply, checks the tank parts and the seal, and tests the flush.

  2. 2

    State the price before work.

    Repair parts and install work are priced clearly before anything begins.

  3. 3

    Repair, reset or replace.

    Small parts are swapped. A leaking base means pulling the toilet to replace the seal and check the flange.

  4. 4

    Test and check the floor.

    The plumber flushes several times and checks the base for moisture. Rotten subfloor, if found, is explained.

Repair or replace

When a new toilet makes more sense

Often a repair is enough. Sometimes the toilet itself has reached the end.

Repair is a good fit when

  • The porcelain is intact and only parts are worn.
  • The toilet is a reasonably modern, efficient model.
  • The problem is the flapper, fill valve or seal.
  • You like the toilet and it fits the space.

Replacement is a good fit when

  • The bowl or tank has cracks.
  • The toilet is very old and uses far more water per flush.
  • Repeated clogs trace to the toilet design.
  • You are remodeling and want a different height or style.

If you are installing a new toilet as part of a remodel, see plumbing installation.

Local conditions

Hard water, pressure and toilet wear in Roy

Weber Basin reports hardness of 10 to 18 grains per gallon for the water it supplies to Roy and nearby cities. Mineral deposits can clog rim jets and make a toilet flush weakly, and deposits can keep a flapper from seating, which makes the toilet run. A vinegar soak can help on some rim jets, but heavy scale needs a plumber.

The city's water plan describes delivery pressure of 60 to 90 psi. High pressure wears fill valves faster than you might expect. If you replace fill valves often, test the pressure at an outdoor spigot, since a regulator may solve the larger problem.

Cost control

Small steps that avoid a larger repair

A toilet is simple, but small problems grow.

  • Do not ignore a running toilet, since it wastes water and signals a worn part.
  • Add a drop of food coloring to the tank. If color shows in the bowl without flushing, the flapper leaks.
  • Never force a loose toilet, since cracking the porcelain turns a repair into a replacement.
  • Use a plunger sized for toilets, not a sink plunger.
  • Keep a small toolkit and a spare flapper for quick fixes.
