Urgent first

A sewer line problem is a health problem too

Sewage backing up into a tub or floor drain is not just a mess. It carries bacteria, and the longer it sits the more damage it does to floors and drywall. Stop using water, keep people out of the area, and call. If water is rising, do not flush toilets or run the washing machine.

Our 24/7 line takes these calls. A repair is a different matter from a cleaning, so the plumber first confirms whether the line is only blocked or actually damaged. That decides whether you need cleaning or repair.

Methods

Trenchless or traditional digging

Both methods fix a broken line. The difference is how much of your yard is disturbed.

Trenchless lining or pipe bursting

  • Fewer excavation holes, so less yard and driveway damage.
  • Suits lines that are fairly straight with moderate damage.
  • Not suited to a collapsed or severely offset pipe.
  • Needs a camera inspection to confirm suitability.

Open-cut repair or replacement

  • Digs down to the pipe and replaces the damaged section.
  • Suits collapsed lines, deep bellies and short repairs.
  • Can restore the original slope accurately.
  • Usually requires restoring the surface afterward.

Local conditions

What affects sewer lines under Roy yards

Roy's housing stock ranges from the early post war years to recent construction, and the lines under it range from clay tile and cast iron to PVC. Older clay and cast iron lines tend to have joints that roots find. Newer PVC lines are smoother but can settle and form a belly where water collects.

The city's own water plan notes that soil in Roy is corrosive to metal pipe. That is a reason many sewer repairs on older metal lines end with PVC. If your home has an older line and you have seen repeat backups, ask for a camera look before any large repair is proposed.

Planning

What to ask before approving a sewer repair

A sewer repair is one of the larger plumbing jobs, so it is worth asking a few questions. Ask to see the camera footage and the location of the problem along the line. Ask why the proposed method fits the damage. Ask what surface restoration is included.

Get the price in writing and confirm what happens if the work uncovers something different. A plumber who stops and explains instead of continuing is protecting you. See our repiping page for a related whole-system decision when pipes throughout the house are aging.

Before help arrives

What to do before help arrives with a sewage backup

Sewage carries bacteria, so treat any backup as a health matter.

  • Stop using every fixture, since all of them feed the same line.
  • Keep people and pets out of the affected area.
  • Avoid touching sewage, and wash hands thoroughly if you do.
  • Open windows to ventilate if it is safe to do so.
  • Photograph the damage for your insurer before cleanup starts.

Leave porous materials like carpet to the cleanup professional, since they can hold contamination.
