Emergency plumber for Clearfield, UT

Clearfield is just south of Roy, across the line into Davis County. Our 24/7 emergency line sends plumbers to Clearfield at any hour. For a burst pipe, shut off the main and call. For a sewage backup, stop using water and call. Give us your cross street and the nearest main road.

Clearfield is shaped by Hill Air Force Base on its eastern side and by the Freeport Center, which began as a Navy supply depot in 1943. Many homes date from the post war and base-growth decades, and a lot of those homes still carry the plumbing they were built with.

Before we arrive

What to do while an emergency plumber heads to Clearfield

Many homes here date from the base-growth years, and their shutoffs may be stiff from age.

1

Turn the main slowly.

An old valve may be stiff or corroded. Turn it steadily and stop if it will not move rather than breaking it.

2

Open one low faucet.

Letting the line drain lowers the volume that can still spill from a break.

3

Clear the floor.

Move boxes and stored items off a wet garage or basement floor, and keep power cords away from water.

4

Call with the nearest landmark.

Give the cross street and, if it helps, whether you are east or west of the main roads near the base.

If you rent, tell your landlord right away. Water shutoffs and shared lines are often their responsibility.

Local water

Clearfield relies on Weber Basin and its own wells

Clearfield City has reported that about three quarters of its water comes from the Weber Basin Water Conservancy District, with the rest drawn from four city wells. Weber Basin lists hardness of 10 to 18 grains per gallon for its water, so most Clearfield taps run hard. Scale shortens water heater life and clogs aerators and rim jets.

If you notice white crust on fixtures, noisy water heaters or low flow at a single faucet, hard water is the usual suspect. See water softener and filtration and faucet repair.
