About us

How Roy 24/7 Plumbing works

Roy 24/7 Plumbing is a plumbing dispatch and service line for Roy, Utah and nearby towns. You call one number, tell us what is going on, and we send a licensed and insured plumber to diagnose it. You get a clear price before any work starts, and you decide whether to go ahead.

We keep the site focused on what matters when a pipe is leaking or a drain is backing up: a number that gets answered, a plain explanation, and a price you approve first. We do not publish plumber names or license numbers on this site, because jobs go to the plumber who is available and qualified for that kind of work. You are always free to ask who is coming and to verify their license.

Our process

What happens after you call

The process is deliberately short.

  1. 1

    You call the line.

    Describe the problem and where you are. If it is an active leak or a sewage backup, we treat it as an emergency and start dispatch right away.

  2. 2

    A plumber diagnoses it.

    The plumber looks at the problem, tests it and explains what is wrong in plain language, including what is optional.

  3. 3

    You get a clear price.

    The price is given before work begins. If you say no, you decide what happens next.

  4. 4

    The work gets done and checked.

    The plumber completes the repair, tests the system and shows you the result before leaving.

If something unexpected turns up mid-job, the plumber stops and explains it before continuing.

Honest limits

What we do not do

We do not do electrical work, roofing, structural repair or drywall finishing, though we will tell you when a plumbing problem needs one of those trades afterward. We also do not claim to be the cheapest plumber in Roy. Our goal is a clear price and a fix that lasts, and we encourage you to compare written prices if you are deciding between companies.

For Utah plumbing licensing questions, the state Division of Professional Licensing runs an online license lookup you can use for any plumber. See our services and service areas for the full scope of what we cover.

Local knowledge

Why Roy plumbing has its own habits

Roy grew from roughly 800 people in the early 1940s to about 24,600 by 1990, according to the Utah History Encyclopedia, so the housing stock spans several building eras. A home from the 1950s, a home from the 1970s and a home from the 2000s each fail in different ways, and a plumber who asks the age of the house before guessing is working with that history.

Roy City runs its own water system from four city wells and treated Weber Basin water, and its 2024 water conservation plan describes delivery pressure of 60 to 90 psi. We use those published facts on this site instead of guessing. Where the city has not published a number for your tap, such as an exact hardness figure, we say so and suggest asking Roy City for its current water quality report.

What to expect

How we handle every call

These are the commitments we hold ourselves to.

  • A person answers the dispatch line, day or night.
  • The plumber diagnoses the problem before any price is given.
  • The price is stated clearly and approved before work starts.
  • If something new appears mid-job, the plumber stops and explains it first.
  • You can ask who is coming and verify their license through the state lookup.
  • Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.
Our service area

Where we send plumbers

We dispatch to Roy, Utah, ZIP code 84067, and to Riverdale, West Haven, Ogden, Clearfield and Layton, with smaller neighboring communities available on request.

Each of those towns has a page that explains the local housing, the water supply and the common problems, because a plumber working in Clearfield and a plumber working in West Haven face different conditions. Our contact page has the phone line and a map of the area.

If your town is not listed, call and ask. Dispatch depends on driving distance and on what is happening that day, not on a line on a map. We would rather tell you honestly whether we can reach you than promise something we cannot keep.

Sources and standards
